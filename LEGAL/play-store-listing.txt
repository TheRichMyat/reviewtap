FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
================================================================

Stop asking customers for reviews. Start collecting them.

Scan & Say creates a custom QR code for your business that takes
customers straight to your Google or Facebook review page in one tap.
No app downloads. No accounts. No friction.

Print it on your receipt, stick it on your counter, or share it digitally.
Every scan is one step closer to a 5-star review.


WHY SCAN & SAY?

★ One-tap reviews — customers go from scan to "Write a review" in
  seconds, not minutes
★ Works on every phone — uses the native camera app, no extra
  download needed
★ Built for small businesses — designed for cafes, salons, mechanics,
  retail shops, and service providers
★ Track what works — see how many people scan your code and click
  through to leave a review
★ Free to use — no subscription, no hidden fees


HOW IT WORKS

1. Sign up in 30 seconds with your email or Google account
2. Add your business name and paste your Google review link
3. Get your custom QR code — share digitally or print it
4. Watch the reviews start coming in

That's it. No setup wizards, no integration headaches, no learning curve.


PERFECT FOR

→ Restaurants and cafes
→ Hair salons and barbershops
→ Auto repair shops
→ Retail stores
→ Cleaning services
→ Fitness studios
→ Any business that depends on word-of-mouth


WHAT YOU GET

• Personal QR code linked to your business
• Branded landing page customers see when they scan
• Download QR as image for printing on receipts, business cards,
  table tents, or signage
• Share QR digitally via text, email, or social media
• Real-time analytics — total scans and review link clicks
• Edit your business info anytime
• Secure cloud storage (powered by Google Firebase)


PRIVACY & SECURITY

Scan & Say collects only what's needed to deliver the service:
your email, business info, and anonymized scan analytics. We never
collect data from your customers when they scan your code. We never
sell your data. Read our full privacy policy at:
https://reviewtap.rivenwebdesign-aiautomation.workers.dev/privacy
