PRIVACY POLICY — ReviewTap

Effective date: May 17, 2026
Last updated: May 17, 2026

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHO WE ARE
----------------------------------------------------------------

ReviewTap ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application that helps small
business owners generate QR codes linking customers to their Google or
Facebook review pages. ReviewTap is developed and operated by Riven
(rivenwebdesign.aiautomation@gmail.com).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use
ReviewTap, how we use it, who we share it with, and what choices you have.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT WE COLLECT
----------------------------------------------------------------

We collect only the minimum information needed to operate the service.

A. Account information (collected when you sign up):
   - Email address
   - Display name (if you sign in with Google)
   - Profile photo URL (if you sign in with Google)
   - Date of account creation

B. Business profile information (you provide this):
   - Business name
   - Google review URL
   - Facebook page URL (optional)

C. Analytics information (collected automatically when customers
   scan your QR code or tap a review button):
   - Event type (scan, Google click, Facebook click)
   - Timestamp
   - Browser user agent (device/browser type)
   - The associated business ID (so we can show you your stats)

We do NOT collect:
   - Customer names, emails, or any personal identifiers from
     people who scan your QR code
   - Location data
   - Contacts
   - Photos or files from your device
   - Microphone or camera content (beyond your phone's native camera
     opening your QR codes — which never sends data to us)

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
----------------------------------------------------------------

We use the information we collect to:
   - Authenticate you and keep your account secure
   - Display your business profile and generate your QR code
   - Show you analytics about scans and link clicks
   - Respond to customer support requests
   - Improve the app and fix bugs

We do NOT:
   - Sell your data
   - Share your data with advertisers
   - Use your data for marketing purposes outside of our own product

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH
----------------------------------------------------------------

We use the following third-party services to operate ReviewTap:

A. Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Hosting)
   Used for: account management, data storage, hosting the customer
   landing page. Firebase is operated by Google LLC and is governed
   by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

B. Google Sign-In (optional)
   If you choose to sign in with Google, your name, email address,
   and profile photo are shared with us from Google.

C. Expo Application Services (development infrastructure only)
   Used to build the app. Expo does not receive user data once the
   app is installed on your device.

We do not share your information with any other third parties unless
required by law (e.g., a court order).

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA RETENTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

   - Account and business profile data is kept for as long as your
     account is active.
   - Analytics data is kept for 24 months, then automatically deleted.
   - If you delete your account, all associated data is removed from
     our active database within 30 days.

----------------------------------------------------------------
6. YOUR RIGHTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

You have the right to:
   - Access the personal data we hold about you
   - Correct inaccurate information
   - Delete your account and all associated data
   - Export your data in a portable format
   - Withdraw consent at any time (which will deactivate your account)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at:
rivenwebdesign.aiautomation@gmail.com

We will respond within 30 days.

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. SECURITY
----------------------------------------------------------------

Your data is stored on Google's Firebase servers and is protected by
Google's industry-standard security practices. We use:
   - Encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS)
   - Encryption at rest (Google Cloud encryption)
   - Firebase Security Rules to ensure only you can access your data

No method of internet transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot
guarantee absolute security but will notify you of any breach affecting
your data within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

----------------------------------------------------------------
8. CHILDREN
----------------------------------------------------------------

ReviewTap is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you
believe a child has provided us with personal information, please
contact us so we can delete it.

----------------------------------------------------------------
9. INTERNATIONAL USERS
----------------------------------------------------------------

ReviewTap is operated globally. Your data may be stored on servers
located outside your country. By using ReviewTap, you consent to this
transfer.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or California,
you have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA. To exercise them,
contact us using the email above.

----------------------------------------------------------------
10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make
material changes, we will:
   - Update the "Last updated" date at the top
   - Notify you within the app on next sign-in
   - Continue to honor the previous policy for data collected before
     the change, unless you accept the new terms
