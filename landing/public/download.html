How to install

  1. Tap the download button above. The APK file will start downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded file (find it in your Notifications or in your Downloads folder).
  3. If Android warns you about "unknown sources," tap Settings → toggle Allow from this source.
  4. Tap Install. The app appears on your home screen as ReviewTap.
  5. Open the app, sign in, and try generating a QR code for your business.
⚠️ Play Protect warning is normal. Beta apps haven't been scanned by Google yet. Tap "Install anyway" or "Don't scan app" — your data is safe.
