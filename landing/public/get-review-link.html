Path A · Recommended if available

Got a verified Google Business Profile?

Use your built-in "Get more reviews" share link — it's the cleanest option.

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account that manages your business.
  2. Pick your business from the list.
  3. Find "Get more reviews" on the dashboard (or "Read reviews" in the left menu).
  4. Click "Share review form" → Copy link.
  5. The link looks like https://g.page/r/CXXXXXXX/review — that's your URL.
✅ Done — skip the next section and paste that link into Scan & Say.
Or

Path B · Works for any business on Google Maps

Use your Place ID

If your business is on Google Maps (even unclaimed), you can build a working review link from its Place ID.

  1. Click the button below — opens Google's free Place ID tool in a new tab.
    Open Place ID Finder
  2. In the search box on Google's map, type your business name and pick the right pin.
  3. Click the pin — a card pops up containing "Place ID: ChIJxxxxxxxxxx".
  4. Copy the Place ID (long string starting with Ch..., about 27 characters).
  5. Paste it in the box below 👇

Can't find your business?
If your business isn't on Google Maps yet, you'll need to add it first. Visit business.google.com and click "Add your business." It's free and takes 5 minutes. Verification (a postcard or video call from Google) can take a few days — once it's listed, come back here.

Last step

Paste your link into Scan & Say

  1. Open Scan & Say on your phone.
  2. If you're setting up for the first time, paste your link in the Google Review URL field. Otherwise: Settings → Edit Business Info.
  3. Save. Your QR is ready to share.
